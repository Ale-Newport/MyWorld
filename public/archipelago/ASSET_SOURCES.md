## Version 2 · compact island redesign

The composition, terrain, road graph, civic fountain, landmark buildings, technology props, coastal layout and browser World Studio were authored locally for this project. `docs/redesign-reference.png` is the image provided by the user and serves as the composition reference.

Additional code copied read-only from the user's Portfolio project:

- `src/world/world/VisualVehicle.ts`, `geometry.ts`, `materials.ts`, and `src/world/core/palette.ts` → `preview/runtime/`.
- `src/world2/World2Game.ts` supplies the actual World2 handling overrides (surface grip, idle brake, damping, boost and low centre of mass).
- `src/world2/World2Environment.ts` supplies the fixed-floor mass treatment required by Rapier's raycast brake impulses.
- `src/world/core/Ticker.ts` supplies the 2:1 simulation/wheel-update ratio.
- `src/world/view/View.ts` supplies the isometric camera parameters and speed response.

The vehicle force, suspension, wheel, reversal and visual implementation are ported code, not a new approximation. Local adaptations are documented in `preview/runtime/driving.js`, `physics.js` and README.md. The immutable source copies are under `assets/source-code/portfolio/`. Existing Portfolio/Bruno Simon attribution and third-party notices above continue to apply.

Three.js TransformControls and GLTFExporter were copied from the same existing installed Three.js package into the local vendor folder (MIT). Shapely and NumPy were installed from PyPI into `scripts/vendor` solely for local road generation and geometric checking; their distribution license files remain in that folder. They are not loaded by the web application.

## World2 functional library · v3

44 source modules copied from Portfolio/src to `assets/source-code/world2-port/` and transpiled into `preview/portfolio/`. SHA-256 provenance is in `reports/v3/source-port.json`; verification is in `reports/v3/source-verification.json`. Includes all interaction controllers, content data, Physics, Player, Inputs, View, Tween, Audio and lifecycle modules. The source project was read only.

Runtime adaptations: local ES module paths, isolated HelloWorld localStorage keys, HelloWorld sea level, production flag, and bitmap lifetime owned by the persistent catalog. Placement and rotation are handled by LocalPhysics outside the original controllers. Original models and colliders come from the already-copied `assets/environment/portfolio/`. The CV PDF referenced in the source profile is absent from Portfolio; it has not been fabricated.

The historical 477-file Portfolio snapshot has four later differences outside this port's 44 source modules. See `reports/v3/portfolio-snapshot-differences.json`; the original historical audit and its failure are retained.
