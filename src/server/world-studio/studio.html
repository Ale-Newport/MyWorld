A world you can change.

Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag to pan. Click selects; Shift + click adds to the selection.

G / W move · R / E rotate · S scale · F focus · ⌘D duplicate · Delete removes · ⌘Z undo · ⌘⇧Z redo.

Experience groups (harbour, frozen lake, circuit…) move as one; Edit Instance enters a group to change its parts. Select a road to edit its points. ⛰ paints and sculpts terrain: create or erase land, raise, lower, smooth, flatten, paint; paths can use the /world2 slab material.

MAP is the top-down 2D editor. DRIVE test-drives the current edit (car, plane, activities) and returns to editing with Esc; nothing the car moves is saved.

SAVE DRAFT stores a draft revision in the portfolio. The admin bar above validates and publishes it to /world and its map; History restores older revisions.

Drive spawn · recovery (inspector): /world starts in the Central Plaza unless Set drive spawn here pins a spawn (validated, undoable, saved with the world). Show recovery anchors draws where a recovery may put the car.
